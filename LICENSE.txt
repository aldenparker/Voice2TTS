WHY GPL
=======

Voice2TTS bundles Piper (piper-tts), which is licensed GPL-3.0-or-later because it
links eSpeak NG for phonemization. Distributing Piper inside this application makes
the combined work subject to the GPL, so Voice2TTS is licensed GPL-3.0-or-later to
match. Earlier builds incorrectly declared MIT; that was a mistake, corrected in
0.4.0.


SOURCE CODE
===========

The GPL requires that you can obtain the source for the version you are running.
The complete corresponding source is available at the project's repository, and the
installed application reports its exact version under Settings -> Updates.
