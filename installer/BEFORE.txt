Voice2TTS
=========

Voice2TTS listens to your microphone, transcribes what you say, and speaks it back
in a synthetic voice through a virtual microphone that Discord, Zoom, OBS and other
apps can use as an input device.

Everything runs locally on your machine. No audio is sent anywhere.


What gets installed now
-----------------------

  * The application
  * Three voices (lessac, amy, ryan)
  * A speech recognition model that runs on your CPU

The app works immediately after install, with no further downloads.


What happens on first run
-------------------------

A short setup wizard offers two optional extras:

  1. A VIRTUAL MICROPHONE. Discord can only hear Voice2TTS through a virtual audio
     device. Windows requires a signed driver for this, so the wizard downloads
     VB-CABLE from VB-Audio and runs their installer. Windows will ask for
     administrator permission, and a restart is needed afterwards.

     VB-CABLE is donationware by VB-Audio. It is not included in this installer and
     is downloaded directly from vb-audio.com. If you already have VB-CABLE,
     VoiceMeeter, Virtual Audio Cable or a similar device, the wizard will find it
     and skip this step.

  2. GPU ACCELERATION. If you have an NVIDIA graphics card, roughly 1.3 GB of NVIDIA
     CUDA libraries can be downloaded to make transcription about twenty times
     faster. This is entirely optional; the app works well on a CPU.

Both can be skipped now and done later from Settings.


A note on the SmartScreen warning
---------------------------------

This installer is not code-signed, so Windows may show "Windows protected your PC".
Choose "More info", then "Run anyway". Signing certificates require a registered
business identity, which this project does not have.
